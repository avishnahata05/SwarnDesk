Build a full-stack Jewellery ERP SaaS web application called "HiraNXT" — 
a modern, AI-powered jewellery business management platform targeting 
Indian jewellers (retail, wholesale, manufacturing). 

Tech stack: React (frontend), Node.js + Express (backend), PostgreSQL 
(database), Tailwind CSS for styling. Use a clean, premium dark-gold 
color theme (#1a1a2e background, #f4c542 gold accent).

--- CORE MODULES TO BUILD ---

1. DASHBOARD
   - Live gold & silver rate ticker (mock live data, update every 30 sec)
   - Daily sales summary cards (sales, purchases, profit, pending orders)
   - Low stock alerts
   - Quick action buttons: New Sale, New Purchase, Add Item, New Customer

2. INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
   - Add items: name, category (gold/silver/diamond/kundan), purity 
     (24K/22K/18K/etc.), weight (gross/net/stone), making charges, HUID 
     hallmark number, barcode
   - Stock in/out with karigar (artisan) assignment
   - Multi-location support (branch-wise stock)
   - Export inventory to Excel/PDF

3. BILLING & POS (Point of Sale)
   - One-screen billing: customer search, item barcode scan, metal rate 
     auto-fill from live rates, GST auto-calculate (3% on jewellery), 
     discount, exchange old gold
   - Print GST invoice (A4 and thermal 80mm)
   - Payment modes: cash, UPI, card, credit, partial payment
   - WhatsApp invoice send button (generate shareable link)

4. CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT (CRM)
   - Customer profile: name, mobile, anniversary/birthday, purchase history
   - Loyalty/scheme tracking (monthly gold saving scheme)
   - Birthday/anniversary auto-reminder
   - Customer ledger (credit/debit balance)

5. KARIGAR (Artisan) MANAGEMENT
   - Issue gold/silver to karigar by weight
   - Track return with wastage % 
   - Karigar-wise pending orders and delivery status
   - Karigar payment/wages tracker

6. REPAIR & ORDER MANAGEMENT
   - Log repair jobs: customer, item description, issue, estimated cost, 
     promised delivery date
   - Status pipeline: Received → In Progress → Ready → Delivered
   - WhatsApp status update button

7. PURCHASE MANAGEMENT
   - Supplier-wise purchase entry
   - Metal receipt: fine gold/silver calculation
   - Bullion trade entry

8. ACCOUNTING & REPORTS
   - Daily cash book, ledger
   - GST reports: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B summary
   - P&L statement, balance sheet (simple)
   - Stock valuation report (at current gold rate)
   - Export all reports to PDF and Excel

9. AI ASSISTANT (differentiator — use OpenAI API or mock it)
   - Chat widget in dashboard: "Ask HiraNXT anything"
   - Answers: "Which items are low stock?", "Show today's sales", 
     "What is my best selling category this month?"
   - Voice input button (Web Speech API)

10. SETTINGS
    - Business profile (name, GSTIN, address, logo)
    - Gold/silver rate manual override
    - User roles: Admin, Salesperson, Accountant
    - Branch management

--- ADDITIONAL IMPROVEMENTS OVER JWELLY ---

- Public reviews/testimonial section on landing page
